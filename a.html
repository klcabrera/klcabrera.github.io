What was caught

The mistakes that were waiting in the documents, and what was done instead.

01May 2025

A $1,180 customer check bounced.

First pass

Delete the payment so the invoice looks unpaid.

Done

Kept the history. Re-billed the store $1,180 plus the $25 returned-check fee, and booked the bank's $12 fee.

02Jul 2025

A $5,000 line on the bank statement just said DEPOSIT.

First pass

Call it income.

Done

Matched it to the savings statement: a transfer between the owner's own accounts. Not income.

03Mar 2025

An invoice's lines added to $455, but the total said $500.

First pass

Enter $500.

Done

Footed the lines, asked the owner, entered $455. The card charge confirmed it.

04Jan 2025

A $61.40 card charge had no receipt.

First pass

Let the bank rule file it and move on.

Done

Counted receipts against card lines, found the gap, asked the owner. It was a re-shipped lost parcel.
