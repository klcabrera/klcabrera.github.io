How a month runs

The same five steps, every month

  1. CollectEvery statement, bill, invoice, payout report and payroll summary, counted against what should be there.
  2. RecordEach document becomes one entry, in the right account, with a memo you can read a year later.
  3. ReconcileEvery bank and card line matched; the difference is $0.00 before the month closes.
  4. CheckClearing accounts at zero; inventory, loan and sales tax tied to outside documents.
  5. Report and askProfit and loss, balance sheet, aging, and a short question list for the owner.
