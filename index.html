How a month runs

Five steps, every month

  1. CollectStatements, bills, invoices, payout reports, payroll summaries.
  2. RecordEach document becomes one entry in the right account.
  3. ReconcileEvery bank and card account, to a $0.00 difference.
  4. CheckClearing accounts at zero; balances tied to outside documents.
  5. ReportP&L, balance sheet, aging, and a short question list.
