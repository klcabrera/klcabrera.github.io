Receivables and payables

Customers billed and collected, vendors paid on time, and the agings always tie to the balance sheet.

Invoices and paymentsBills and bill paymentsCredit memosVendor creditsBounced checksWrite-offsAging reports

How it's done

  1. Record each invoice and bill from the document, never from the bank line.
  2. When something goes wrong (a bounced check, a damaged order, a price correction) use the form that leaves a trail; never delete.
  3. Watch the aging every month and act early: a reminder at 30 days, a question to the owner before any write-off.
