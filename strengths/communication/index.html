Client communication

Short, plain-English questions to the owner, sent with each month's report and followed up until they close.

Monthly question listOwner reportCollection remindersYear-end memo

How it's done

  1. Never guess silently: record the most likely treatment, say so, and ask.
  2. Keep each question to one sentence the owner can answer from her phone.
  3. Follow up: an open question stays on the list until it is answered.
