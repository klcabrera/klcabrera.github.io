Bank and card reconciliation

Every bank and card statement matched to the books, every month, with a $0.00 difference.

Bank reconciliationCredit card reconciliationOutstanding checksDeposits in transitTransfers between accounts

How it's done

  1. Start from the statement: the ending balance is the target, not the books.
  2. Match every line, leave timing items unticked, and fix anything that is really an error before closing.
  3. Prove it in Excel too: the workpaper rebuilds each reconciliation from the general ledger and must show a 0.00 difference.
