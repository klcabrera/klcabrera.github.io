Month-end and year-end close

A month-end close that runs the same way every time and ends with a closing date, not a guess.

DepreciationPrepaid expensesInventory from the countAccrualsLoan scheduleClosing date

How it's done

  1. Post the recurring entries: depreciation, the insurance that was used up, cost of goods sold from the month-end count.
  2. Check what must be zero (clearing accounts, Opening Balance Equity) and tie inventory, the loan and sales tax to outside documents.
  3. Close with a date and a password, so nothing in a reported month can change without a trace.
