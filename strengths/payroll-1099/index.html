Payroll entries and 1099s

Payroll recorded from the provider's summary, wages tied out at year-end, and 1099s based on what was actually paid.

Payroll journal entriesNet pay clearingPayroll tax liabilitiesAnnual wage tie-outW-9 tracking1099-NEC

How it's done

  1. Book each payroll from the provider's summary as one journal entry: wages and employer taxes in, net pay and taxes owed out.
  2. Keep the clearing account at zero: each cash withdrawal for payroll clears what the entry set up.
  3. At year-end, tie wages to the provider's annual total, and list 1099s by payment date, checking each W-9 classification.
