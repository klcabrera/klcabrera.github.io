E-commerce books

Online sales booked from the payout report, so sales, refunds, fees and sales tax are each right, not just the deposit.

Shopify payoutsClearing accountMerchant feesRefunds and discountsSales tax payablePeriodic inventory

How it's done

  1. Record each payout from its report: gross sales, shipping, sales tax, refunds, discounts and fees, through a clearing account.
  2. The bank deposit then clears the clearing account to zero, so the deposit is never booked as sales.
  3. Each month, sales tax payable must equal the tax report, and inventory must equal the count.
