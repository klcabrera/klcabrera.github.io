The lines that look wrong

May: Lone Star's $1,180.00 check bounces; bank fee $12.00. Never delete the payment. Invoice Lone Star: Bounced Check item $1,180.00 (points to Checking) plus Returned Check Fee $25.00; expense the $12.00 bank fee.
May: Duplicate ShipLine $96.50, refunded 6 days later. Record both charges and the refund; all three are ticked in the card reconciliation.
May: Cinemark $42.75 on the debit card. 3200 Distributions, credit Checking.
July: DEPOSIT $5,000.00 in checking on July 14. It is a transfer from savings (the savings statement shows the same $5,000.00 going out). Record one transfer, Savings to Checking. Not income.
July: Lone Star's $1,205.00 is past due. A/R aging shows it 31 to 60 days late: send a polite reminder.
