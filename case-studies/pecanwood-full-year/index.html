Found and fixed

Problems in the documents that nobody pointed out. Five examples:

May

Lone Star's $1,180.00 check bounces; bank fee $12.00

Kept the original payment, then re-billed the customer for the check plus the $25.00 returned-check fee.

July

DEPOSIT $5,000.00 in checking on July 14

Matched it to the savings statement: a transfer between the company's own accounts, not sales.

May

Duplicate ShipLine $96.50, refunded 6 days later

Recorded both charges and the refund, so the card reconciliation ties.

February

Marisol's first invoice, no W-9 on file

Asked the owner for the W-9 before the next payment; it arrived in March.

December

December electricity, billed January 8, 2026

Booked December's power bill in December ($441.29), although it arrived in January.

Questions to the owner

A short list goes with each month's report. Three examples:

To the owner · January

The Visa shows a $61.40 ShipLine charge on January 23 with no receipt in the folder. What was it for? Could you forward the receipt?

To the owner · May

Lone Star Pet Pantry's $1,180.00 check came back unpaid on May 21 (the bank charged $12.00). I've re-billed them $1,205.00, including our $25.00 returned check fee. Would you like to call them, or should I email them?

To the owner · September

Lone Star is now over 90 days past due on $1,205.00 and hasn't answered two reminders. Do you want to keep trying, or should we write it off?
