Questions to the owner

One short list goes with each month's report: 14 questions in the year.

To the owner · January close · mine

The Visa shows a $61.40 ShipLine charge on January 23 with no receipt in the folder. What was it for? Could you forward the receipt?

To the owner · February close

Marisol Vega's first invoice came in, but we have no W-9 for her. Could she complete one before we pay the next invoice? We need it for her 1099 next January.

To the owner · March close

BoxCraft's March invoice lines add up to $455.00, but the total says $500.00, and the card was charged $455.00. Which amount is right?

To the owner · April close

There's an $85.40 H-E-B charge on the Visa on April 12 with no receipt. Was it business or personal? I've recorded it as a distribution for now.

To the owner · May close

Lone Star Pet Pantry's $1,180.00 check came back unpaid on May 21 (the bank charged $12.00). I've re-billed them $1,205.00, including our $25.00 returned check fee. Would you like to call them, or should I email them?

To the owner · May close

There's a $42.75 Cinemark debit card charge on May 3 with no receipt. Business or personal?

To the owner · June close

Lone Star still owes $1,205.00 from the bounced check. Shall I send them a polite reminder in early July?

To the owner · July close

Lone Star Pet Pantry still owes $1,205.00 from May. I'll send them a polite reminder this week. Do you have a contact name there?

To the owner · August close

Lone Star hasn't answered July's reminder. Shall I send a firmer one asking for payment by September 15?

To the owner · September close

Lone Star is now over 90 days past due on $1,205.00 and hasn't answered two reminders. Do you want to keep trying, or should we write it off?

To the owner · October close

Saltmarsh's October order is $21,820.50, our largest this year. I'll pay it on the due date unless you'd like it paid sooner. OK?

To the owner · November close

Rosa Jimenez is on payroll from November 10. Is she seasonal through December 31 only, so I can plan December's payroll?

To the owner · December close

Capital City Power will bill December's electricity around January 8. Please forward the bill as soon as it comes so I can include it in December.

To the owner · December close

Your loan repayment of $10,233.33 went out December 29: $10,000.00 plus $233.33 interest (4% for 7 months). Does that match your note?
