What December needed

Maya's loan repaid $10,233.33. Dr 2500 Loan from Shareholder 10,000.00; Dr 8000 Interest Expense 233.33; Cr Checking 10,233.33. 2500 ends at 0.00.
December electricity, billed January 8, 2026. The bill is in the year-end packet. Accrue it in December: Dr 6351 Electricity 441.29, Cr 2400 Accrued Expenses. January 2026's bill then goes to 2400.
Outstanding check and deposit in transit at December 31. Morales & Kent check 1129 ($350.00) and the December 31 deposit ($1,304.00, The Whisker Barrel) are not on the December statement. Leave them unticked.
$50,000.00 to savings; $15,000.00 distribution. The transfer prints as DEPOSIT on the savings statement: it is a transfer, not income. The distribution goes to 3200.
Year-end count. Inventory is adjusted to the count like every month.
December electricity bill in the year-end packet. Accrue $441.29 in December before you close it (see the December key).
Marisol's 1099 amount. 1099-NEC reports what you PAID in 2025: $7,140.00. Her December invoice ($760.00) is paid in January 2026, so it belongs on her 2026 1099, not this one.
Who gets a 1099-NEC. Jordan Reyes, Lindqvist Design Studio (single-member LLC), Marisol Vega. Not Lanternfly or Morales & Kent (corporations), not Tom Becker (under $600), not suppliers of goods.
Wages. Officer Wages plus Staff Wages on the P&L equal Cedar's annual gross wages.
